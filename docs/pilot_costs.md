# Pilot costs — method and sources

Estimate for the 17-week shadow pilot described in section 4 of the proposal
(3 weeks set-up, 13 weeks pilot, 1 week review). Team members are assumed to be
A352 employees, so the cost is the employer cost of their time, not a consultancy rate.

| Role | Annual gross salary, Luxembourg 2026 | Employer cost per day* | Working days | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data / back-end engineer (bank and accounting connections, engine hardening, back-tests) | €65–80k, mid-point €72.5k | €369 | 25 | €9,227 |
| Software developer (turns the prototype into the product, with the warning card inside Financial Navigator) | €60–78k, mid-point €69k | €351 | 10 | €3,513 |
| Product owner (priorities, pilot coordination, CFO feedback) | €75–105k, mid-point €90k | €458 | 10 | €4,582 |
| Data protection officer / security engineer (security review, impact assessment) | €65–80k, mid-point €72.5k | €369 | 5 | €1,845 |
| **Total** | | **average €383 per day** | **50** | **≈ €19,170** |

\* Employer cost per day = annual gross salary × 1.12 (employer social contributions, about 12% of gross in 2026) ÷ 220 working days per year.

Not included: the pilot CFOs' time (about one hour a week each, borne by their own companies) and the
language model (under €1 per client per month). The module runs on Financial Navigator's existing infrastructure.

**Assumptions (ours):** the number of working days per role; the use of salary mid-points; 220 working days per year;
cybersecurity analyst salaries used as a proxy for the data protection / security role.

## Sources

- Developer, data engineer and cybersecurity salaries: SalaireClair, *Salaire informaticien au Luxembourg 2026* (9 May 2026), based on Hays, Michael Page and Randstad — https://salaireclair.fr/blog/salaire-informaticien-luxembourg-2026
- Product owner / product manager salaries: All Eyes On Me, *Salary guide for Luxembourg in 2026* — https://luxembourg.alleyesonme.jobs/en/articles/salary-guide-for-luxembourg-in-2026-how-much-do-people-really-earn-in-finance-it-and-human-resources
- Employer social contributions (about 12% of gross, pension 8.5% from 1 January 2026): Teamed, *Luxembourg cost breakdown* — https://www.teamed.global/country-hiring-guides/luxembourg/cost-breakdown
